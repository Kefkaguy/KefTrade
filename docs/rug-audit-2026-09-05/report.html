Repair the experiment before scaling the search

The audit found reproducible errors in forward P&L attribution, cost coverage and intraday execution. Repairs are implemented locally. Five profitable strategies have not been established.

The five existing candidates are historical leads. Their VPS records currently report zero paper orders, zero paper fills and insufficient_forward_sample. The repaired search still needs to run against the full frozen dataset.

Scope: candidate generation, data loading, backtesting, costs, learning and paper evidence. This is a focused research-path audit, not an exhaustive security or UI review. No production deployment, campaign change or broker trade was performed.

Data observations and limits

The latest 1,000 rolling 15m rows for each requested symbol had no duplicate timestamps and carried the alpaca_iex label. The AAPL sample had 115 outside-session rows, 34 complete regular sessions and one truncated first session. Other symbols had different latest timestamps. This supports using explicit session filters; it does not certify the full frozen dataset or establish that every regular session is bad.

Regular market hours and early closes should follow the NYSE calendar. IEX and consolidated SIP have different coverage, so source labels matter for both price and volume evidence. Alpaca explains the feed differences.

What would establish cost survival?

A larger search also creates more chances to select historical noise. The original Deflated Sharpe Ratio research by Bailey and López de Prado explains why trial counts and selection bias matter. Reusing an old holdout after learning from it does not restore independence.

Paper execution checks implementation, but it does not establish live fills or costs. Alpaca's paper model omits impact, latency slippage, queue position and regulatory fees; KefTrade's separate internal simulator also requires its own validation. Alpaca paper-trading specification.

Before accepting five strategies, the remaining work is representative execution-cost measurement, protected final/prospective evaluation, multiplicity correction, parameter-neighborhood robustness, sufficient independent observations, aligned correlation and shared-capital checks, corporate-action/feed validation, and short-borrow/capacity evidence where applicable. These gates remain incomplete. No stop-after-five certification was invented.

A concrete next experiment

The new app.cli.rug_research runner can audit the existing five without updating their records, or test a bounded 15m/five-asset pilot. It verifies frozen data before generation, releases its read-only database connection before simulations, journals results, and uses complete valid cohorts for the next batch's feedback. Technical errors do not become trading losses.

It distinguishes frictionless losses, baseline-cost failures and stress failures, and measures throughput. Its development shortlist is not a certified portfolio. The full local database connection timed out, and the public candle endpoint is capped at 1,000 rolling rows, so this real-data pilot has not been run.

VPS build, audit, pilot and monitoring commands. The runbook requires the patched checkout on the VPS. Building its standalone research image does not restart the currently running trading services.
